About the data

Source
Simulated teaching dataset. Values are long-term monthly climate normals for each city (published climate tables for Cairo, Alexandria, Port Said, Hurghada and Aswan) with small random variation added to imitate a single year. It is not an official observation record.
Period
January to December 2025, one record per city per month (dataset prepared November 2026).
Records
Fields
date, city, temperature (monthly mean, °C), humidity (mean relative humidity, %), rainfall (monthly total, mm), wind_speed (mean, km/h), condition (predominant condition of the month).
Calculations
Average = sum ÷ number of valid values. Highest / lowest = largest / smallest monthly mean. Total rainfall = sum of the monthly totals of the selected cities and months. Condition share = months with that condition ÷ all selected months × 100. °F = °C × 9/5 + 32.
Cleaning
Empty cells and impossible values (humidity outside 0–100 %, negative rainfall or wind, temperature outside −50 to 60 °C) are set to “missing”, shown as “—” in the table and left out of every calculation.
Limitations
Monthly means hide daily extremes, so “highest temperature” is the hottest month, not the hottest day. Five cities cannot represent all of Egypt, and simulated variation means single values should not be quoted as real measurements.
